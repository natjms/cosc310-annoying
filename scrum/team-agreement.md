### Part 2 — Team Agreement

#### Team Values

Three core values: integrity, communication, reliability. We will work without AI/LLM assistance, we will have strong and open inter-team communication, and we will be able to rely on each other for help.

#### Meetings
Lab attendance is mandatory. No additional meetings, only meeting at the lab. We will take notes at each lab on the progress of our project & anything team-related. If someone cannot attend, they will write a message via Signal to indicate this, and we will catch them up next lab. They can follow along at home after the lab using the notes.

#### Communication

We will communicate via our already-existing Signal group chat. Signal is an instant messaging app that is available for most devices. All information will be shared via Signal.

#### Response Expectations
Same-day responses during weekdays, weekends usually off unless something is due & the team agrees. Will respond to important messages within 8 hours. If a team member fails to respond: it will be reflected on their peer review. If a team member cannot contribute, the rest of the team will organize to take over their responsibilities.Respect & Professional Conduct We are all adults here, and we have all had experience doing high-quality group work in an academic environment before. Respectful conduct comes easy to our team.

#### Accountability

As per the response expectations, team members that are not respectful, do not contribute or communicate, or otherwise fail to meet expectations, will have their behaviour reflected in their peer review.

#### Decision Making and Conflict Resolution

If we are at an impasse or other disagreement on the direction of our team or project, we will decide what to do via Signal poll. If there is a complete disagreement, we will try to find middle ground, and the team can elect to play a Source, GoldSrc, or Quake-engine FPS deathmatch game to decide the outcome of the disagreement.

#### Anticipated Challenges

- Tooling disagreement, what software to use and what is compatible
	- Solve with normal methods
	- We will try to use widely compatible tooling
- Mismatch in the formatting of the code
	- Use .editor format
	- Maybe use linter?
- Loss of project context between labs
	- Some sort of note/log book and logging in the Signal group chat
- Missed deadlines
	- We will set personal deadlines sooner than the actual deadlines by one to two days
